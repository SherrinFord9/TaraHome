Before you commit

What stays outside the starting estimate.

Tara reviews these decisions with you before confirming the order. No specific product model, delivery date, or support duration is promised by the example above.

Cameras and storage

Additional camera coverage and local recording need their own device, storage, placement, and cabling plan.

Installation work

The standard kit is self-install: Tara prepares the system and checklist; you place and power the devices. Electrical wiring, HVAC changes, and difficult cable runs are reviewed separately and may need a qualified professional.

Rental restrictions

Tell Tara what you can change. Keep shared entrances and building-managed heating outside your plan unless their use is agreed. Removable sensors and compatible lamps may be a better starting point than replacing fixed hardware.

Existing products

Send exact model numbers for devices you want to keep. Compatibility and available features need review; an existing device is not automatically a supported replacement or a guaranteed discount.

Final purchase terms

Confirm the itemized product list, shipping and any taxes, delivery timing, warranty or return terms, and the support scope before accepting the quote. Optional third-party services can have separate fees.

FAQ

Quick answers.

What changes the final price?

Home type and device counts change the online planning estimate. Exact models, compatibility, optional cameras, and the installation boundary determine the confirmed quote. The $2,999 example is not a fixed price for every one-bedroom home.

Does this include a required subscription?

No required monthly subscription is needed for core local routines. Optional third-party cloud features may have fees. Batteries, replacement devices, and separately agreed support are not a promise of free lifetime maintenance.

Is this an order or a planning request?

The configurator sends a planning request. It does not charge you or place an order. Tara confirms the product list, installation responsibilities, final price, delivery timing, and support terms with you before purchase.

Can I use the starter kit in a rental?

A rental can be planned around removable devices. Check permission before changing a thermostat, doorbell, switch, or mounting surface, and exclude shared-building systems you do not control. Tell Tara which devices must move with you.

Are exterior cameras included in the $2,999 example?

No. The example includes a doorbell but zero additional camera zones. Exterior cameras, recording storage, and any cabling are reviewed separately through the camera-kit path.
